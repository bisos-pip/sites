* Key Design Decisions
  - Platform/sites separation: bisos.platform and bisos.sites are being
    deliberately decoupled. Where overlap existed (platformBases_csu.py's
    siteBase), the code was *copied* into sites_csu.py, not moved -- the
    platform package's copy is left untouched/unmodified, out of scope.
  - bisos.sites.sites_csu is now the single, complete owner of
    /bisos/var/sites and /bisos/site symlink management going forward;
    bisos.platform's siteBase is legacy/parallel and known-incomplete.
  - platfSiteBootstrap.py was explicitly excluded as a target for the
    site_lib.sh conversion (per user direction) -- do not add site-symlink
    Cmnds there.
  - A new skill, icm-to-pycs-conversion, documents the general Bash-ICM ->
    PyCS mapping (vis_* -> cs.Cmnd, FN_fileSymlinkUpdate ->
    pathPlus.symlink_update, FN_absolutePathGet -> bpo.bpoBaseDir_obtain,
    etc.) since ~55 more *_lib.sh files remain under
    /bisos/core/bsip/bin/ as candidates for future conversion.
